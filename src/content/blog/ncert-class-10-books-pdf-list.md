## How to use NCERT for Class 10 boards

- **Science and Maths:** finish every in-text question and exercise. Board papers follow the NCERT style closely.
- **Social Science:** make a one-page summary per chapter with dates, terms and map items. Maps are easy marks.
- **English:** know each story's theme and characters; the long answers are about those, not about memorising lines.
